# All-in-One Video & Music Downloader

A free, lightweight userscript that adds a download button on supported video and music sites. No extra software is required.The script does not dynamically load third-party libraries; the code is neither encrypted nor obfuscated. It is open to public scrutiny, so you can use it with confidence.

The userscript and all download sources are completely free! Download supported content without limits, including YouTube videos in 4K, 2K, 1080p, and other available resolutions. Enjoy high-quality downloads at no cost!

## How to Download

- Click the download button on any supported video or music track page. The script will automatically open [GrabShorts](https://www.grabshorts.com/en) and pass the current URL for processing.
- Once the preview dialog appears on GrabShorts, you'll see a long download button at the bottom of the dialog.
- The button displays the download status, including the start prompt, download progress, and final result.
- To cancel an ongoing download, simply click the button again.
- Once the download is complete, a ✓ check mark will appear before the button text. The button color remains unchanged.
-Downloads go through a third-party site. That site may show a human verification that lasts about 10 seconds. You can complete the verification the site suggests, or wait until it finishes.

PS： The third-party website(Grabshorts) may show a human verification that lasts about 10 seconds. You can complete the verification the site suggests, or wait until it finishes. Third-party websites are not controlled or operated by this userscript. When using the script, never share or disclose any personal or sensitive information on third-party websites, including but not limited to your identity details, payment information, or other private data.

## Third-Party Website Selection Criteria

We carefully select third-party download websites based on the following strict criteria:
- **Completely Free**: The website must provide its download services entirely free of charge, with no hidden fees or paid subscriptions required.
- **Legal and Safe**: The website must not engage in any illegal activities or provide unlawful services.
- **Ad-Free**: The website must be completely free of advertisements, banners, and intrusive advertising.
